## Reason for Recall

The mold growth with loss in colour was observed in the bottles. Also the fizz appeared in few bottles. So as a precautionary measure, all the bottles were recalled and stored in a warehouse. The manufacturer and consultant were immediately informed about the same. Since there was no positive response from their side regarding the possible reason we put hold on sales of further batch that was manufactured subsequently and still holding the same in our warehouse.
