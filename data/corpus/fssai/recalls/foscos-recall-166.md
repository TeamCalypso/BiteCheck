## Reason for Recall

Unsafe - The Food Analyst analysed the above said sample and issued Form VII A Report
as per reference cited (2) is of the opinion that the said sample contains added
synthetic food colours Tartrazine (Colour Index: 19140) and Sunset Yellow
FCF (Colour Index: 15985) of which Tartrazine (Colour Index: 19140) is not
permitted in Snacks as per Table 15 of Appendix A, Food Category System
15.1 of Food Safety and Standards (Food Products Standards and Food
Additives) Regulations, 2011 and is therefore Unsafe under Section 3(1)(zz)(v) &
(vii) of Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006.
