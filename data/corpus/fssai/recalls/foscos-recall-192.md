## Reason for Recall

Misbranded - The product recall process was initiated on 08.07.2026 due to a misprinting issue on the 350 g High Protein Probiotic Dahi cups.This product is distributed through only two distributors. Both distributors were immediately informed to stop the sale and distribution of the affected batch. Subsequently, our marketing team collected 18 boxes of the affected product from the market and returned them to the manufacturing plant.

We have informed the Food Safety Commissioner about the voluntary recall of the misprinted High Protein Probiotic Dahi cups. The submission of the detailed recall status report is currently in progress. The report will include the recall status, an action plan for the utilization/disposal of the returned products, and corrective and preventive actions (CAPA) to ensure that such an incident does not recur in the future.
